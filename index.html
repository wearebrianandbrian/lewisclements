    Hi, I’m Lewis

    I’m a multi-award-winning Senior Creative and Trailer Editor, with over 10 years’ experience editing, directing and crafting trailers and campaigns for TV, film and social.

    I work across creative and edit, and I direct too, leading shoots with high-profile talent for shows like The Great British Bake Off, Married at First Sight UK, The Piano and Late Night Lycett. That means I can take a brief from the first idea all the way to the final cut, from the hero trailer to every last social version.

    Along the way I’ve made award-winning launch trailers, teasers and social campaigns for some of the UK’s biggest broadcasters and entertainment brands.
